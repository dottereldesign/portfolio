# Laptop mockup source

The five laptop angles are adapted from [Mockups Design's "Free MacBook Air 2022 mockup" pack](https://mockups-design.com/free-macbook-air-2022-mockup/).

The source page identifies the pack as free for private and commercial use with no attribution required. The exported portfolio assets have transparent backgrounds, custom screen artwork, and AVIF/WebP delivery formats. The original PSD files are not redistributed in this repository.
